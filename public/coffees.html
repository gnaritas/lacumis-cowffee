Hailey taught herself the long list of Mexican-inspired drinks drink by drink. These are the ones the trailer became known for — every one made to order, hot or iced.

Full Menu Get Directions

1Mazapán Latte

Named for mazapán — the classic Mexican peanut-and-sugar candy that melts into espresso and steamed milk. Caramel, peanut, a little crunch of memory. The cup people drive to the trailer for.

2Banana Latte

Espresso and milk with banana — naturally sweet, smooth, and the one first-timers order when they don’t want to gamble. Order it iced in August, hot in January.

3Mexican Mocha

Mexican coffee and chocolate — the table at abuela’s house, with espresso behind it. Not a syrup punch: coffee-forward, chocolate-deep.

4Coconut Matcha & Cookie Butter Matcha

Two matchas for two moods: one whipped with coconut, light and tropical; the other swirled with cookie butter, dessert-in-a-cup territory. Both hot or iced.
