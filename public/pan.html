The Panadería

Pan made the slow way:
by hand, in small batches

The breads came before the coffee. What started as empanadas sold from the family kitchen is now the counter the café opens with — conchas, gorditas, croissants and whatever else the season asks for.

Full Menu Get Directions

Conchas

Soft, slightly sweet yeast bread under a crackled sugar shell — the panadería signature, the one kids point at through the case.

Gorditas

Fresh masa cakes, split and stuffed — the weekend-baking staple that built the Facebook following.

Croissants & empanadas

Empanadas are the origin story — sweet-potato empanadas with her mom, sold from home because people kept asking for more. Croissants joined the weekend repertoire as demand grew, and both stay on the counter.

Seasonal bakes

Follow along on Instagram — pan de muerto arrives around Día de Muertos, pumpkin conchas run in the fall, and drops sell out.
