The story so far

From middle-schooler empanadas to a storefront

Hailey Hernandez started baking as a kid — sweet-potato empanadas with her mom, then a home bakery on Facebook, then a weekend route of gorditas, conchas and croissants. In 2024 the family bought a food trailer and painted it cow-print. Now, at 21, she’s opening her first real café.
